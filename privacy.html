Privacy Notice

ONs' Fantasy Rugby Manager

Plain English summary: We collect your name and email address to run the fantasy rugby competition. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than running the competition and keeping you informed about it. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Who we are

This privacy notice applies to the ONs' Fantasy Rugby Manager competition, run by Old Northamptonians RFC ("the club", "we", "us"). The competition is operated as a club activity for paid-up members.

For data protection queries, contact us at treasurer@onrfc.co.uk.

What data we collect

Competition managers (participants):

  • Name and email address (required to create an account)
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by you)
  • Your team selections, transfers, and points throughout the season
  • Payment status (whether your entry fee has been paid)

Players:

  • Name and position (from club records)
  • Match statistics (appearances, tries, etc.) recorded by scorers
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by club administrators)
  • Sponsor details (if a sponsor has been assigned to a player)

Sponsors:

  • Business name, contact name, email, phone number
  • Invoice and payment records

Why we collect it and our legal basis

We collect and use your personal data under legitimate interests — specifically, the legitimate interest of running an organised club competition for our members. We only collect what is genuinely necessary for this purpose.

  • Name and email — to create your account, contact you about the competition, and send push notifications you opt in to
  • Profile photo — to display your identity on the leaderboard and team pages, visible to other participants
  • Match stats — to calculate fantasy points and run the competition fairly
  • Payment status — to verify eligibility to participate

Who sees your data

  • Other competition participants — your name, photo, team name, and fantasy points are visible to all managers in the competition
  • Club administrators — can see all data necessary to run the competition
  • Supabase — our database provider, hosted in the UK (London). Data is stored securely and not accessed by them for any purpose other than providing the service
  • Nobody else — we do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party for marketing or any other purpose

How long we keep your data

Active participants: Your data is kept for the duration of the competition and for two years after the season ends, to allow for historical records and any queries.

After two years: Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, photo) are removed and replaced with anonymised references. Match statistics and points history are kept for the permanent season archive.

Sponsor invoicing records are kept for six years in line with standard accounting requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data (the "right to be forgotten"). You can request this directly in the app via More → Profile → Request account deletion, or by emailing us
  • Objection — object to our processing of your data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email treasurer@onrfc.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Cookies and tracking

This app does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. We use browser local storage only to remember your login session and display preferences (such as text size). No third-party trackers or analytics tools are used.

Push notifications

If you choose to enable push notifications, your device token is stored by OneSignal (our notification provider) to deliver notifications about the competition. You can withdraw consent at any time via More → Profile → Notifications in the app.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated via the app inbox. The date at the bottom of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact us

For any data protection queries or to exercise your rights:

treasurer@onrfc.co.uk
